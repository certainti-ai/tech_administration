## The request

We respectfully request that the delay in processing the proposed adjustments be extended from **August 31, 2026 to September 30, 2026**. This letter confirms in writing our telephone discussion with you on [DATE OF CALL], in which this request was raised.

## Why the additional time is needed

**1. Five of the seven projects were adjusted on documentation grounds only, and the documentation requires assembly across multiple systems.** Projects 3 through 7 received no eligibility review. Per the Audit Adjustments working paper, they were adjusted because the salary and hour breakdowns requested in the letter of May 7, 2026 were not provided. Producing records in the form your working paper describes — a breakdown of monthly hours for each employee claimed, indicating SR&ED and non-SR&ED activities for each project — requires extraction and reconciliation of time, resource-management and payroll data across a delivery organisation operating from several locations. We would rather provide records that reconcile to the amounts reported on the T661 than provide a partial extract that raises further questions.

**2. Contractor documentation must be retrieved and verified before it is submitted.** The arm's-length contract expenditures reported on line 340 ($304,752) require the underlying agreements and invoices, an allocation of hours to each project, and evidence going to the taxable-supplier status of the ultimate performers. These records sit with procurement and finance and must be verified before we put them in front of you.

**3. The line 435 adjustment flows from another taxation year.** The revision of line 435 from $467,727 to $175,850 is based on the investment tax credits as reassessed for the 2023-03-31 taxation year. We are confirming the status and particulars of that reassessment so that our representations for 2024-03-31 are consistent with our position on the prior year.

**4. A single complete submission serves both parties.** We would prefer to file one organised package, cross-referenced to the findings in the Project Eligibility Report and to your Audit Adjustments working paper, rather than to submit material in instalments.
