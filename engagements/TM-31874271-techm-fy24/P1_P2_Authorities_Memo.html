The authorities

Lead with A and C. They are the Advisor's own program policy, and he has to engage with them. Everything after D belongs at objection stage.

A

System uncertaintyCRA, SR&ED Glossary · Eligibility of Work for SR&ED Investment Tax Credits Policy (2015) · Guidelines on the eligibility of work (2021)

Binding on the RTA
Proposition
a form of technological uncertainty that can arise from or during the integration of technologies, the components of which are generally well known … due to unpredictable interactions between the individual components or sub-systems.

CRA adds that it may be impossible to predict how the integrated system will perform because of unforeseeable adverse interactions, and that the uncertainty lies not in the individual components but in their behaviour as an integrated system.

Why it matters

This is the direct answer to the finding that both projects were the “application and integration of established … tools” and “integration of existing technologies”. CRA's own policy says integration of well-known components can generate technological uncertainty. The Advisor's finding is not wrong as a general proposition, but it is incomplete as stated, and this is the policy that completes it.

The gate

Read the next sentence of the policy before relying on this. CRA requires that the specifications of the integrated system be such that the underlying technologies of the sub-systems cannot achieve them, so that some or all of those underlying technologies had to be changed. The policy also directs the reader to distinguish work that advances a sub-system's underlying technology from work that does not.

Wiring tools together does not clear this. For P2 you must show Appium and Selenium were incapable of the handoff as shipped and what you had to alter. If the answer is a polling loop built on documented APIs, this authority defeats you rather than assists you.

B

Quality control and routine testing are excludedIncome Tax Act, s.248(1) SR&ED definition, para (f) · CRA guidance on quality control and routine testing

Binding · adverse
Proposition

The definition excludes quality control and routine testing. CRA treats quality control as work sustaining the quality of a product or service to satisfy given requirements, and routine testing as procedures to observe, measure or verify attributes during normal business operations. The stated rationale is that such work does not result in technological advancement.

Why it is the deepest problem

Both projects are testing engagements on their face — P1 validates call detail records against known expected attributes, P2 is a quality engineering managed service by contract. The first submission described faster validation, a “golden dataset” verifying that expected fields were correctly represented, and automation coverage. Every one of those is a description of quality control, and the Advisor's determination reads as though he applied this paragraph without needing to name it.

The only way past

The instrument distinction. Executing tests against given requirements is excluded; developing a means of measurement that did not exist, where it was uncertain whether the measurement could be made at all, is experimental development, and the testing is its application. Every argument advanced must be framed that way or it walks into this paragraph.

C

Support work attaches to eligible workIncome Tax Act, s.248(1) SR&ED definition, para (d)

Binding on the RTA
Proposition

Engineering, design, operations research, mathematical analysis, computer programming, data collection and testing are within the definition when commensurate with the needs of, and directly in support of, eligible work under paragraphs (a) to (c).

Why it matters

It is the answer to “this is just scripting”. Automation development does not have to be experimental development in itself — it qualifies as support work provided an eligible core exists for it to support. That reframes a large share of the claimed effort, and it is how the smaller candidates (P1-D in particular) get claimed at all.

The gate

Support work is parasitic. Without an identified, eligible core activity there is nothing for it to attach to, and the whole claim collapses to excluded testing. The representation must name the core explicitly rather than leaving the Advisor to infer it — which is precisely what the first submission failed to do.

D

Northwest Hydraulic Consultants Ltd. v. The Queen98 DTC 1839 (TCC) · Bowman J · taxpayer successful · citation to be verified

Foundational · supports
Proposition

The source of the five questions the Advisor is applying. Bowman J set out the framework for technological uncertainty, advancement and systematic investigation that CRA subsequently adopted into policy, and courts continue to apply it notwithstanding CRA's 2021 restatement in “how and why” terms.

Why it matters here
  • The taxpayer won, and was an engineering consulting services firm doing modelling work for clients — a closer structural analogue to Tech Mahindra than a product company. Services delivery is not a bar.
  • If the Advisor frames his test in the newer “how and why” language, this establishes that the five questions remain the operative standard and that a services firm can satisfy them.

E

DAZZM Inc. v. The King2024 CCI 129 · Gagnon J · heard Montréal 27–28 March 2024 · judgment 8 October 2024 · docket 2021-3161(IT)G

Recent · verify first
Facts

Development of version 5 of an IT management platform using React, Redux, styled components and higher-order components. CRA had partly denied the 2018 claim, disallowing one of two projects under s.248(1).

Holdings
  • Combining known components can itself create technological uncertainty where the interactions between them are unpredictable — judicial endorsement of what authority A says as policy.
  • Uncertainty is assessed at project level, not task level. Useful against a determination that dismisses activities one at a time, which is close to how the PER reads.
  • Performance metrics and time estimates were accepted in place of daily timesheets. This reaches the substantiation problem, not just eligibility — and on this file that may be worth more than the eligibility holdings.
Before you cite it

Commentary describes the hearing as informal and therefore not precedent-setting. The docket recorded is 2021-3161(IT)G, where the “G” denotes General Procedure. Those cannot both be correct and we could not reach the judgment to resolve it. If it is General Procedure it carries real weight; if informal it is persuasive only and must be described as such. Settle this before the memo leaves the building.

F

Clevor Technologies Inc. v. The QueenTCC · Russell J · judgment 14 August 2019 · software · taxpayer unsuccessful · citation to be verified

Adverse — know it first
Holding

The claim failed on three grounds: no technological uncertainty was identified; the approach taken was trial and error, described as a technique known to professionals competent in the field and not accepted as a valid SR&ED methodology; and there was no evidence of testing results.

The exposure

This is the case that decides P1-B and P2-A if the artefacts come back the wrong way. If the timing buffer was adjusted until the failures stopped, that is trial and error and Clevor is the answer to it. Same for a synchronisation mechanism arrived at by successive guesses.

The distinction that survives is between iteration and systematic investigation: a sequence of attempts each of which tested a stated prediction and produced a conclusion that shaped the next is not trial and error. A sequence of adjustments until symptoms disappeared is. The commit history decides which one happened, and it will not be flattered by advocacy.

04

How to deploy these, and where

The same authority carries different weight depending on who is reading it. Getting this wrong wastes the strongest material at the stage where it counts least.

With the Research and Technology Advisor — policy only

Lead with A and C, and frame everything through the instrument distinction so B never gets a clean run. An Advisor applies the Income Tax Act and his own program's policy; he is not bound by the Tax Court and will not be argued out of a technical determination by citation. What can move him is being shown that his own guidance recognises the category of uncertainty we are describing — and then being given the artefacts.

Do not put case law in front of him as the main argument. Advocacy at the technical stage reads as a claimant who has run out of facts, and this file cannot afford that impression twice.

At Notice of Objection or Appeals — the case law earns its keep

Appeals officers weigh authority properly. D establishes the framework and that a services firm can satisfy it; E, if it survives verification as General Procedure, is the closest recent authority on both integration-based uncertainty and on accepting substitutes for daily timesheets. That second holding may matter more than the first, given where this file's real weakness is.

The objection right arises only on reassessment and runs 90 days from it. Nothing here needs to be spent before then.

Three things not to argue

Do not argue that other companies get credits for test automation. It is unprovable, it invites the answer that each claim turns on its own facts, and it concedes that we are arguing from comparison rather than from evidence.

Do not cite foreign authority as though it carried weight. UK and US regimes apply different statutory tests — the US four-part test under IRC §41 and its process-of-experimentation requirement are not the Canadian test. Mentioning them signals weakness in the domestic position.

Do not restate the section 244 mechanisms under cover of a citation. No authority substitutes for the artefact. The Advisor's finding was that the mechanisms were not demonstrated, and that finding is answered with records or not at all.

05

What has to be true for any of this to work

Authority A is the whole strategy, and it has a condition

Every argument in this memo routes through system uncertainty, and CRA's own policy conditions it on the sub-system technologies being unable to meet the integrated specification, such that they had to be changed. So the question the artefact hunt actually has to answer is narrower than “did we do something clever”:

What could Appium, Selenium, LeanFT — or the CDR ingestion path — not do as shipped, and what did we change about them? If the answer is that we composed them as designed and handled the seams, this memo does not save the claim, and the honest course remains the one already on the file.
